LANDLORD REVIEW CONTEST PROCESS

We recognise that landlords and property managers who are the subject of Reviews have a legitimate interest in ensuring that Reviews about them are accurate, fair, and comply with our Terms. This section sets out the formal process by which landlords may contest Reviews they believe to be false, defamatory, misleading, or otherwise in violation of these Terms.

Eligibility to Contest a Review

A Review may be contested by: (a) the landlord, property owner, or property management company who is the direct subject of the Review; (b) an authorised representative acting on behalf of the landlord or property owner, with appropriate written authorisation; or (c) a legal representative acting in their professional capacity on behalf of the landlord or property owner.

Grounds for Contesting a Review

Reviews may only be contested on one or more of the following grounds:

Factual Inaccuracy: The Review contains statements of fact that are demonstrably false and the contesting party can provide evidence to prove the falsity of such statements.

Defamation: The Review contains defamatory statements that are false and harmful to the reputation of the landlord or property.

Mistaken Identity: The Review relates to a different landlord or property and has been posted in error on the wrong listing.

No Tenancy Relationship: The reviewer was never a tenant of the property or had no legitimate tenancy relationship with the landlord being reviewed.

Conflict of Interest: The Review was posted by a competitor, a party with a personal vendetta, or someone with a material conflict of interest that was not disclosed.

Violation of Review Guidelines: The Review violates our Responsible Use Policy and Review Guidelines as set out in these Terms, including but not limited to containing harassment, discrimination, illegal content, spam, or private personal information.

Fraudulent or Malicious Review: The Review was posted fraudulently, maliciously, or as part of a coordinated campaign to damage the reputation of the landlord.

How to Submit a Review Contest

To formally contest a Review, the contesting party must submit a Review Contest Request to us at info@rentradar.co or through the designated dispute resolution portal on our Website.

The Review Contest Request must include: the full name and contact details of the contesting party; proof of identity and, where applicable, proof of authority to act on behalf of the landlord or property owner; clear identification of the Review being contested, including the URL or unique identifier of the Review; a detailed explanation of the grounds on which the Review is being contested; supporting evidence; a signed declaration that the information provided is true and accurate to the best of the contesting party’s knowledge and belief; and the specific remedy sought.

Review Contest Process and Timeline

Upon receipt of a complete Review Contest Request, we will conduct a preliminary review of the contest, notify the original reviewer if the contest proceeds, and endeavour to reach a decision within thirty (30) business days of receiving all necessary information from both parties. Both parties will be notified of the outcome in writing.

Possible Outcomes

Following our review, we may take one or more of the following actions: No Action; Removal of Review; Partial Redaction; Addition of Context or Correction; Landlord Response; or Reviewer Sanctions.

Appeal Process

Either party may appeal the decision within fourteen (14) days of receiving notification of the outcome. Appeals must be submitted in writing to info@rentradar.co and must clearly set out the grounds for appeal and any new evidence not previously considered. The appeal decision shall be final and binding, subject to any rights either party may have under applicable law.
